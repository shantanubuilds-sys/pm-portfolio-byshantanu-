Electrical Engineering student · Aspiring Product Manager

Learning, building, and documenting the process

I want to understand not just how a product works, but why it should exist.

My engineering studies give me a foundation for understanding technical systems. Through product concepts and AI experiments, I'm learning to connect that technical perspective with user problems, prioritization, product decisions, and measurement.

This portfolio is a working record of that journey. Some work is at prototype stage, some is early exploration, and some is a prompt experiment. I try to make the evidence and the gaps visible rather than dressing assumptions up as results.
